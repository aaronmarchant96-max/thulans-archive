# The Thulans Archive
### *Stories of work, shared warmth, and the people inside the machinery of Faírguni.*

> *"In the mountain city of Fairguni, water is rationed by blood. Forty days after nineteen-year-old Wulfila surrenders his life for his family's water allowance, his younger brother Bram finds Wulfila's brass chit hidden in a rail coupling. Guided by Wulfila's instructions, Bram and the drift smith Kaelen retrieve a survey slate from his locker: **the North Cistern was full two days before Wulfila died.**"*

---

## 🗺️ Explore the World

Human civilization survives inside a continent-sized hollow volcanic mountain, measuring space vertically: **Up-Mountain**, **Down-Mountain**, **Coreward**, and **Rimward**.

* **[[The Mountain|🗺️ Canonical Cross-Section Map & Sector Directory]]**: View the architectural blueprint of the summit ark yards, the heavy foundries, the timber flues, Lower Four, and the rising magma encroachment front.
* **The Living Corpse Economy**: 4.2 million living Thulans inhabiting an arcology engineered for 42 million at its technological peak.
* **The Hidden Clock**: While the ore lasts 30–50 years, rising magma will breach the deep sumps in 11–28 years.

## 📖 The Five Core Strata

1. **[[The Mountain#1-the-summit--the-spire-elev-3200m-to-4500m|The Summit & The Spire]]**: High gantry cradles for the six atmospheric exodus arks, Theodemir's Tier Nine Council balcony, and the Ministry of Allocation.
2. **[[The Mountain#2-the-upper-works--foundries-elev-1800m-to-3200m|Foundries & Heavy Works]]**: Blast furnaces, rolling mills, Foundry Bay Nine, and the Great North Aqueduct.
3. **[[The Mountain#3-the-timber-flues--mid-levels-elev-600m-to-1800m|Timber Flues & Mid-Levels]]**: The 40,000-strong Timbermen Guild, bracket-fungus farms, and haulage skip terminals.
4. **[[The Mountain#4-lower-four--the-deep-hearths-elev--400m-to-600m|Lower Four & Deep Hearths]]**: Shaft 44 (banded magnetite breach site), Drift 14, Azg-Haíms, and the children's communal nursery.
5. **[[The Mountain#the-encroachment-clock|The Magma Intrusion]]**: The rising volcanic floor driving the Council's secret evacuation timeline.
